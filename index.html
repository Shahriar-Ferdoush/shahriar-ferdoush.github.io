Research

Model merging and task-vector selection

Paper under review · 2026

Merging task vectors is the cheap alternative to multi-task retraining: it composes capabilities from checkpoints that were fine-tuned independently, at the cost of an addition. My current work is on deciding which entries of a task vector to keep as the drop rate rises, and on what a merged model loses when those decisions are made badly.

The method matches the state of the art at standard drop rates and pulls clearly ahead at aggressive ones, holding its accuracy at drop rates of 90% and above where existing methods degrade sharply.

Supervised by Dr. Mohammed Eunus Ali (Associate Professor, Monash University) and Dr. Saddam Mukta (Postdoctoral Researcher, LUT).

A paper from this work is under double-blind review, so I am keeping the specifics off this page until the review period ends — I am glad to discuss it directly, get in touch.

Enhancing Optical Coherence Tomography with Modified Deep Image Prior for Improved Denoising via Early Stopping Convergence

Undergraduate thesis, BUET · 2024

Deep Image Prior denoises from a single noisy observation by using the network architecture itself as the prior, with no clean training data. Its weakness is knowing when to stop: run too long and the network fits the noise. I modified the early stopping criterion to detect that crossover sooner, reaching better denoising performance in far fewer iterations, and implemented the method independently in both PyTorch and TensorFlow.

The question underneath it — when has a network fit signal rather than noise, and which parameters carry which — is the same question that sent me toward model merging.
